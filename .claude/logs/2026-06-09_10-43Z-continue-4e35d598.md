## Epic â€” Authentication & Application Shell

Here's what this epic builds:

**1. BFF session proxy and auth foundation** *(under-the-hood â€” verified by step 2)*
Under-the-hood wiring that lets the app talk to the login service securely and remember who you are during your visit. No screen of its own â€” it powers the sign-in and sign-out steps that follow.

**2. Login screen with credential and connectivity error states**
As an Importer or Approver, you enter your email and password on the sign-in screen and sign in. If your details are wrong you see a clear "wrong email or password" message; if the service can't be reached you see a separate "can't connect, try again" message with a retry. A privacy-policy link is visible on the screen.

**3. Role-based landing routing and route protection**
After you sign in, you land on the right home screen for your role â€” an Importer goes to the Dashboard, an Approver goes to the Transactions screen. If you are signed out and try to open any app page, you are sent to the sign-in screen first. If you open a page your role can't use, you see an in-page "you don't have permission" message naming what's missing â€” not an error page.

**4. Application shell with role-gated navigation and sign-out**
Once signed in, you see the app's top/side navigation showing only the screens your role is allowed to use, your name or email, and a Sign out control. Signing out returns you to the login screen and you can't get back in by pressing Back. A privacy-policy link is available in the shell.

**5. Session lifecycle â€” idle warning and timeout handling**
If you leave the app idle, you get a 60-second warning before being signed out, and you can stay signed in by acting on the warning. After long inactivity, or once your session has run its full length, you are signed out and returned to the login screen with a clear explanation.
